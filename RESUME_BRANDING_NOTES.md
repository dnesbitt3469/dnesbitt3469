# Résumé Branding & Editing Notes

## Shared Professional Identity

Both résumés must communicate four central strengths:

1. Client and stakeholder management
2. Complex workflow coordination
3. Analytical problem-solving
4. Enterprise-system experience and technological adaptability

Preserve demonstrated sales excellence, employee development, leadership, and business operations experience.

## LinkedIn Résumé — Purpose

**Primary goal:** Present an accomplished, versatile business professional who is credible for sales, customer success, account management, operations, legal support, and technology-adjacent positions.

- Emphasize professional relationships, negotiation, leadership, business responsibilities, and measurable results.
- Demonstrate experience managing demanding institutional clients, complex procedures, and software systems.
- Use concise descriptions that communicate the scope of each position.
- Preserve both leadership and individual-contributor appeal.
- Maintain the two-page hybrid structure, with Relevant Professional Experience and Additional Professional Experience.

## GitHub Résumé — Purpose

**Primary goal:** Demonstrate how established business, analytical, and operational abilities translate into technology-oriented work.

- Emphasize systems thinking, structured problem-solving, technical adaptability, and continuous learning.
- Include relevant technology skills and evidence from actual projects.
- Use DavidOS Builder and other documented work to demonstrate technical initiative.
- Preserve career achievements and transferable experience without overstating technical qualifications.
- Link technical claims to demonstrable work wherever possible.

## Consistency Rules

- Employment dates, employers, education, awards, and contact information must agree across both documents.
- Different wording and emphasis are encouraged; contradictory claims are not.
- Do not invent metrics, software proficiency, job titles, or accomplishments.
- Avoid generic corporate jargon and inflated descriptions.
- Preserve specialized experience when it demonstrates transferable skills.
- The LinkedIn résumé should complement, not duplicate, the GitHub résumé.

## Editorial Standard

**Same accomplished professional. Two complementary perspectives.**

LinkedIn establishes business credibility and professional value.

GitHub supplies additional evidence of technical capability, initiative, and applied problem-solving.
